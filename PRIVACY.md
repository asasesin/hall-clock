# Privacy Policy — Hall Clock

**Applies to:** the Hall Clock Android app (`com.nuxcor.hallclock`) and the Hall
Clock appliance software.

**Last updated:** 7 August 2026

## Summary

Hall Clock collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no servers of ours for data to be sent to.

## What the app is

The Android app is a thin shell around a web page served by a Hall Clock
appliance — a small computer installed on the same local network as your phone.
All of the app's functionality comes from that device. There is no online
service behind it.

## Data we collect

**None.** We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal or
device information. Specifically, the app does not collect names, email
addresses, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash
reports, or advertising identifiers.

## Data stored on your device

Two things are saved locally on your phone, and neither leaves it:

- **The clock's address** — the network name or IP of the appliance the app
  connects to, so you are not asked for it on every launch.
- **A pairing token** — issued by your own appliance so the controller does not
  have to be re-authorised each time it opens. It is meaningful only to that
  device on your network.

Both are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Network access

The app requests internet permission solely so it can reach the Hall Clock
appliance over your local network. It makes no connections to any other host.
Traffic stays on your own network and does not reach us or any third party.

## Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting
libraries.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
regardless of age.
